                        About Platform Atlas
                        
        
                        
          
                        A way into a large codebase. Explore its modules as a city, follow a source walkthrough, then open the files behind the map.
                        
          
                        
            Project on GitHub
            @doniv01
            Project overview
          
                        
          
                        Each atlas is a curated snapshot pinned to one source revision. The map does not measure production traffic or prove that a feature is enabled; follow the file links to check the code yourself.
                        
          
                        Find your bearings
                        
          
                          
            
                        • Map: select a tower to open its source panel. Files lists every path at the pinned revision.
                          • 
            
                        • Modules: browse every module by type or district, or search when you know what you need.
                          • 
            
                        • Walkthrough: read a chapter at a time, or step through the individual calls.
                          • 
            
                        • Pan / Orbit / Move: slide the city, turn around it, or drag a tower or district. Scroll or pinch to zoom toward the cursor.
                          • 
            
                        • Angle & layers: drag the compass or use its arrow keys, snap to Front, Top, or Side, and show districts, modules, or both.
                          • 
            
                        • Share: copy a link that reopens the selected module, or save a PNG of the 3D view.
                          • 
            
                        • Settings: choose the Luminous, Matte, or Architectural finish. The finish and any saved view stay in this browser.
